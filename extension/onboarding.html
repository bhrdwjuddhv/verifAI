Right-click anything. Nothing leaves your machine.

VerifAI checks whether an image was AI-generated or manipulated, and shows you which detector said so. The models are bundled and run here — there is nothing to sign up for, nothing to configure, and no upload.

How to use it

  1. Right-click any image and choose Verify with VerifAI.
  2. A badge appears on the image. Click the toolbar icon for the full breakdown.
  3. For video — YouTube Shorts, Reels — the toolbar popup has Scan the video playing here, because those players swallow the right-click menu.

What a verdict actually means

A "real" verdict means the detectors found nothing — not that the file is authentic. Accuracy against generators the models have never seen is not yet measured, and confidence is uncalibrated. Every scan shows which detectors voted, their weights and the model that produced them, so you can judge the number rather than trust it.

A detector that cannot apply — no face in the picture, say — abstains and says so. It is never replaced by a guess.

The one time something is uploaded

Scoring a whole video means sampling frames across it, which only the VerifAI server does. When that is the better answer the extension offers it — and asks first, naming where the file is going. You can decline and keep the single-frame result.

Self-hosting? Point it at your own deployment in options, and make deep scan the default there if you prefer.
